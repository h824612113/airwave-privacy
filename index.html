Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 29, 2026

English 中文

This policy explains what the AirWave Android application ("the App", "we", "us") does with information when you use it. AirWave is an internet radio player. It has no user accounts and asks you for no personal details.

The short version: we don't collect your name, email, phone number or location. Your favourites and listening history stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what our advertising and payment providers need to do their job.

1. Information we do not collect

AirWave does not ask for, and does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, videos or files
  • Audio recordings — the App only plays audio, it never records it. The App does not request microphone permission.
  • Any account credentials, because there are no accounts

2. Information stored on your device only

Your favourite stations, recently played list and app preferences are saved in the App's private storage on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

If you have enabled Android's system backup feature, your device may include this data in your own Google account backup. That backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by us.

3. Information processed when you use the App

Station search and browsing

Station data comes from radio-browser.info, a free community-run public database. When you browse or search, your query and your device's IP address are sent to that service so it can return results. This is a normal web request; we do not store the results of these queries. See the radio-browser.info project site for its own practices.

Advertising

The free version of AirWave displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android AD_ID)Serving and measuring ads
IP address, device type, OS version, general device settingsAd delivery, fraud prevention
Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks)Ad performance measurement

This data is shared with Google and its advertising partners. We do not receive your advertising ID ourselves and we cannot identify you from it. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms.

You can limit ad personalisation at any time:

  • Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → delete or reset your Advertising ID
  • Within the App, watch a rewarded video for 60 minutes ad-free, or purchase the ad-free upgrade

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card number, billing address or any payment credential. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase is valid, so the App knows to hide ads. Google's handling of payment data is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service.

5. Children's privacy

AirWave is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App requires no registration and collects no personal details, there is no personal profile for a child to create. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will address it.

6. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar)

Because we hold no account and no personal profile for you, there is generally no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete. The favourites and history on your device are removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

For the advertising data described in section 3, the controller is Google. You can exercise your opt-out and deletion rights through the Android ad settings linked above.

If you are in the EEA, UK, Switzerland or California and wish to make a request anyway, contact us at the address in section 8 and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

7. Data retention and security

We operate no server that stores your personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain. All network traffic between the App and its service providers uses HTTPS/TLS. On-device data lives in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially — for example, if we add a new third-party SDK — we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google Play Billing — payments
  • radio-browser.info — station directory data

Individual radio stations are operated by third parties. We do not control their content or their practices. Audio streams are fetched directly from the station's own server, which will see your IP address as any web request would.

隐私政策（中文）

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 20 日

本政策说明 AirWave 安卓应用（下称「本应用」）会如何处理你的信息。 AirWave 是一款网络收音机播放器，没有账号体系，也不会向你索取任何个人资料。

一句话版本：我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、手机号或位置信息。 收藏和收听记录只保存在你的手机上。唯一会离开设备的数据， 是广告和支付服务商为了完成它们的工作所必需的那部分。

我们不收集什么

姓名、邮箱、电话、住址；精确或大致位置；通讯录、日历、照片、视频、文件； 录音（本应用只播放音频，不录音，也未申请麦克风权限）；账号凭据（因为没有账号）。

只存在你设备上的数据

收藏的电台、最近播放列表和应用偏好设置，都保存在本应用的私有存储里， 不会上传给我们或任何第三方。清除应用数据或卸载应用即会删除。 如果你开启了安卓系统的备份功能，这些数据可能被包含在你自己的 Google 账号备份中 —— 该备份由你和 Google 控制，与我们无关。

使用过程中会处理的数据

  • 电台搜索与浏览：电台数据来自 radio-browser.info 这一免费的社区公开数据库。浏览或搜索时，你的查询词和 IP 地址会发送给该服务以返回结果。 这是普通的网络请求，我们不保存这些查询结果。
  • 广告：免费版由 Google AdMob 提供广告。 为实现广告投放与效果衡量，Google Mobile Ads SDK 会收集和处理广告标识符 （Android AD_ID）、IP 地址、设备型号、系统版本、设备设置， 以及广告展示与点击事件。这些数据与 Google 及其广告合作伙伴共享。 我们自身不会拿到你的广告标识符，也无法据此识别你的身份。 你可以在「安卓设置 → 隐私 → 广告」中删除或重置广告 ID， 也可以在本应用内观看激励视频换取 60 分钟无广告，或购买去广告版本。
  • 购买：订阅与一次性购买完全由 Google Play 结算处理。 我们看不到也不会存储你的卡号、账单地址或任何支付凭据， 只会收到「购买有效」的确认，以便隐藏广告。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会在知情情况下收集儿童的个人信息。 由于本应用无需注册、不收集个人资料，儿童也无从建立任何个人档案。

你的权利

由于我们不持有你的账号或个人档案，通常没有可供查询、更正、导出或删除的个人数据。 设备上的收藏和记录，通过清除应用数据或卸载应用即可移除。 第 3 节所述的广告数据，其控制者是 Google，你可以通过上述安卓广告设置行使退出与删除权。
